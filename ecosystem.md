# [`Craig A. Wendorf`](./README.md)

## My Project Ecosystem

The areas below are connected parts of a larger effort to make psychology research and teaching more transparent, practical, and reusable. They support different stages of analysis, from expressing a research comparison to interpreting results and communicating evidence.

### Social and Cultural Psychology

These packages use computational approaches to examine social and cultural patterns.

- [wvsR: Cross-Cultural Comparisons Using WVS Data](https://github.com/cwendorf/wvsR)
- [dsitR: Dynamic Social Impact Simulator](https://github.com/cwendorf/dsitR)

### Teaching and Learning

These teaching materials help learners move from course goals to meaningful practice. They connect educational design to practical statistics instruction.

- [Sourcebook: Basic Statistical Methods for Social Science](https://github.com/cwendorf/Sourcebook)
- [BASE: Basic Analytical Statistics in Excel](https://github.com/cwendorf/BASE)

### Estimation and Inference

A central theme in my work is describing effects with estimates, confidence intervals, and effect sizes rather than relying on hypothesis testing alone.

- [EASI: Estimation Approach to Statistical Inference](https://github.com/cwendorf/EASI)
- [backcalc: Reconstruct Missing Inferential Statistics](https://github.com/cwendorf/backcalc)
- [DEVISE: Data Exploration, Visualization, and Inference with Statistical Estimation](https://github.com/cwendorf/DEVISE)
- [spTools: Additional Functions for the statpsych Package](https://github.com/cwendorf/spTools)
