## My Project Ecosystem

These four areas are connected parts of a larger effort to make psychology research and teaching more transparent, practical, and reusable. Social and cultural questions give the work its subject matter; statistical tools help investigate those questions; teaching resources make the methods approachable; and developer utilities reduce friction in building and sharing the work.

### Teaching and Learning

My teaching work is about helping learners move from course goals to meaningful practice. It connects educational design, clear explanations, opportunities to apply ideas, and thoughtful assessment, while drawing on research methods and substantive questions in psychology.

- [teaching: My Teaching Materials](https://github.com/cwendorf/teaching): Materials and resources for my courses, including teaching, studying, and grading approaches. **[Website]**
- [BASE: Basic Analytical Statistics in Excel](https://github.com/cwendorf/BASE): A collection of Excel spreadsheets for teaching statistics through real-time calculations and graphs. **[Excel Spreadsheets]**
- [Sourcebook: Basic Statistical Methods for Social Science](https://github.com/cwendorf/Sourcebook): An online textbook connecting introductory statistics concepts with practical analysis in SPSS, jamovi, JASP, R, and EASI. **[Website]**

### Statistics and Research Methods

My statistical work aims to make quantitative reasoning understandable and useful, with an emphasis on estimation, uncertainty, visualization, and clear reporting. Its parts support different stages of analysis, from expressing a research comparison to interpreting results and communicating evidence.

- [Modified stripChart with a Swarm Option](https://gist.github.com/cwendorf/a4e487bcb8e8f751610a536fa96d1e4a): A modified base R strip chart that adds a swarm display option. **[R Code]**
- [summarizeAnova](https://gist.github.com/cwendorf/15ec1c42d3c6e3a7170ce01cbb6799f3): Summarize an ANOVA or deviance table in a tidy data frame. **[R Code]**
- [summarizeModel](https://gist.github.com/cwendorf/26ce206dbbac17fd1bc888f127ad1bee): Summarize model terms, coefficients, standard errors, test statistics, p-values, and confidence intervals in a tidy data frame. **[R Code]**
- [Estimate p Value from CI](https://gist.github.com/cwendorf/c7698c9f8e132e94bb49d34e36e525ff): Estimate an approximate p-value from a difference and its confidence interval. **[R Code]**
- [Estimate CI from p Value](https://gist.github.com/cwendorf/e752ab6d1589a42f0ab131d2e7b638e9): Estimate an approximate confidence interval from a difference and its p-value. **[R Code]**
- [Minimalistic Gardner-Altman Plot](https://gist.github.com/cwendorf/d467a13398efb21c9ad1e0fa3ae1b2ba): Create a simple Gardner-Altman plot for a two-group mean comparison, with options for confidence intervals and a region of practical equivalence. **[R Code]**
- [plotDistributions: Visualizing Probability Distributions](https://github.com/cwendorf/plotDistributions): An R package for plotting probability density and cumulative distribution functions, with tools for highlighting probabilities and critical values. **[R Package]**
- [polyPlot: Visualizing Distributional Statistics](https://github.com/cwendorf/polyPlot): An R package for visualizing distributional summary statistics with a modified polyplot. **[R Package]**
- [CALM: Contrast Algorithms for Linear Models](https://github.com/cwendorf/CALM): An R package for creating and interpreting contrast codes in linear models. **[R Package]**
- [spTools: Additional Functions for the statpsych Package](https://github.com/cwendorf/spTools): An R package of additional functions for `statpsych`. **[R Package]**
- [EASI: Estimation Approach to Statistical Inference](https://github.com/cwendorf/EASI): An R package for describing, estimating, testing, and visualizing confidence intervals across common statistics and research designs. **[R Package]**
- [DEVISE: Data Exploration, Visualization, and Inference with Statistical Estimation](https://github.com/cwendorf/DEVISE): An R companion package for preparing data and presenting statistical results as accessible summary tables and comparison plots. **[R Package]**
- [backcalc: Reconstruct Missing Inferential Statistics](https://github.com/cwendorf/backcalc): An R package for reconstructing standard errors, confidence intervals, test statistics, and p-values from incomplete summary data. **[R Package]**

### Social and Cultural Psychology

My psychology work uses empirical and computational approaches to examine social and cultural patterns. Its substantive interests include how values vary across societies and how interaction can shape shared beliefs; each approach contributes a different kind of evidence.

- [wvsR: Cross-Cultural Comparisons Using WVS Data](https://github.com/cwendorf/wvsR): An R package for uncertainty-aware comparisons and cultural profiles using the EVS/WVS Joint Dataset. **[R Package]**
- [dsitR: Dynamic Social Impact Simulator](https://github.com/cwendorf/dsitR): An R package for simulating social influence among agents with multidimensional opinions. **[R Package]**

My developer work supports research by making R code easier to reuse and project materials easier to maintain. Their purpose is practical and enabling: they improve the workflow around substantive methods rather than serving as substitutes for those methods.
